# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mixed warehouse and office staff at Burdens. Warehouse staff check and process orders on the floor — speed and status legibility matter most. Office staff manage order queues, filter by store or status, and administer user accounts from a desk. Both audiences are non-technical; the interface must work without training.

## Product Purpose

Burdens SOF (Shopify Order Fulfillment) is an internal operations tool that surfaces Shopify orders and their fulfillment status, bridging Shopify with the Frameworks ERP. Staff use it to monitor order state (success / failed / pending), identify issues quickly, and manage who has access. Success means zero ambiguity: at a glance, any staff member knows what needs attention.

## Brand Personality

Precise, reliable, navigational. The tool should feel like it knows exactly what it's doing and never wastes the user's time. Not cold — considered. Three words: **clear, confident, grounded**.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS aesthetic: cream/warm-neutral backgrounds, gradient text, hero metrics, marketing-style chrome, glassmorphism as decoration
- Heavy enterprise clutter: SAP-style panels, sidebars within sidebars, too much visual noise
- Consumer/playful apps: rounded bubbles, bright pastels — doesn't fit a logistics context

## Design Principles

1. **Status at a glance** — order state must be immediately scannable; no hunting, no hover-to-reveal.
2. **Earn every pixel** — every UI element must do a job. If it can't be explained in one word, reconsider it.
3. **Confidence over decoration** — precise spacing, clear hierarchy, and quiet authority over ornamental chrome.
4. **Accessible by default** — WCAG AA throughout; the tool works for every staff member on any screen.
5. **Consistent over creative** — a stable, predictable system beats clever one-offs that create support burden.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Full keyboard navigation for all interactive elements. Sufficient color contrast for all text and status indicators (do not rely on color alone for order status — always pair with a text label). Reduced-motion fallbacks for any animations.
